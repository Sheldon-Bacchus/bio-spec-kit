# Bioinformatics analysis plan

Read `spec.md`, the constitution, and existing `.bio/` contracts. Resolve
tool-specific questions through documented research. The plan must cover the
pipeline engine, environment, references, QC thresholds, statistical model,
resource profile, provenance, failure handling, and human review gates.

Do not silently choose thresholds or statistical contrasts. Mark unresolved
decisions explicitly and identify the evidence needed to resolve them.
